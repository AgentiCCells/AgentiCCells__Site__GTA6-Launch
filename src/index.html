GTA 6 launch: the facts, with sources

Unofficial. This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Sony or Microsoft. Names are used only to describe the game. Every fact below links to where it came from and says when we checked it.

The short answer

Grand Theft Auto VI was released on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC version.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 out?

Yes. Rockstar's game page says "Now Available", and the Rockstar Store gives a release date of 19 November 2026. (rockstargames.com/VI and Rockstar Store, checked 20 November 2026.)

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not announced. Rockstar lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Anything else is rumour until Rockstar says so.

Is it on PS4 or Xbox One?

Not announced. Rockstar lists only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Does the boxed copy have a disc?

No. Rockstar says physical versions contain only a download code, so you still need to download the game.

What time does it unlock?

We could not confirm unlock times on a Rockstar page. Your console's store page for the game shows the time for your region.

What age rating does it have?

ESRB Mature in the US. Other rating boards set their own ratings.

What if a fact here is out of date?

Each fact shows the date we checked it. Rockstar's own pages are the authority; if they differ from this page, trust them.
